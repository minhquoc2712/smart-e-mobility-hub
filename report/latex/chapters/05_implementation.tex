\safesubsection{Important Implementation Decisions}
\label{subsec:impl-decisions}
\Cref{tab:impl-decisions} summarizes the six technically critical engineering mechanisms implemented to guarantee state consistency, concurrency safety, and cross-device usability.

\begin{table}[H]
  \centering
  \singlespacing\footnotesize
  \caption{Key Technical Implementation Decisions Enforcing Concurrency and Consistency}
  \label{tab:impl-decisions}
  \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.24}
  \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|L{3.5cm}|Y|}
    \hline
    \rowcolor{tblHeader}
    \textbf{Engineering Challenge} & \textbf{Implemented Technical Solution} \\
    \hline
    Preventing Concurrent Double-Booking \& Deadlocks & Every reservation transaction acquires InnoDB \texttt{SELECT ... FOR UPDATE} locks in a strict canonical order (\texttt{users} $\rightarrow$ \texttt{reservations} $\rightarrow$ \texttt{vehicles} $\rightarrow$ \texttt{hubs} $\rightarrow$ \texttt{parking\_\allowbreak slots}). Locking the \texttt{users} row first acts as a per-account mutex under \texttt{REPEATABLE READ} (\texttt{BR-01}), while interval overlap predicates block cross-user double-booking. \\
    \hline
    Role-Safe 5-Stage Approval \& Cancellation Attribution & Operator rejections invoke the dedicated operator reject endpoint (\texttt{OPERATOR\_\allowbreak REJECTED}), whereas customer cancellations invoke the student cancel endpoint, which unconditionally records \texttt{STUDENT\_\allowbreak CANCELLED} or \texttt{STUDENT\_\allowbreak DECLINED}---ensuring accurate status attribution across both portals. \\
    \hline
    Concurrent Multi-Charger \texttt{AUTO} Port Allocation & Defaulting the charger/parking selector to \texttt{AUTO} computes shaded calendar hours from the intersection of \emph{all} operational ports at a hub. Two students booking the same hour at a 2-free-port hub atomically lock distinct ports (\texttt{BK-C02}, \texttt{BK-C03}), while subsequent requests enter \texttt{QUEUED}. \\
    \hline
    Two-Phase Slot-to-Slot Fleet Redistribution & Vehicle dispatch executes in two atomic phases inside one transaction---Phase A vacates all source slots to \texttt{FREE} before Phase B binds destination slots to \texttt{OCCUPIED}---enabling safe intra-hub swaps and inter-hub transfers while filtering out private customer EVs. \\
    \hline
    Strict In-Memory What-If State Isolation & Simulation runs deep-clone live relational rows into isolated heap memory with a seeded PRNG; live tables are modified only if an operator confirms a recommendation that passes fresh live-capacity revalidation. \\
    \hline
    Mobile Wi-Fi/LAN Sync \& Clock-Skew Resilience & Dynamic hostname resolution binds mobile Safari/Chrome over campus LAN (\texttt{192.168.1.x:\allowbreak 5173}) to the REST API (\texttt{:4000}) with $4\text{ s}$ live polling, while client-anchored pickup timestamps prevent PC-to-phone clock skew from stalling the elapsed ride timer. \\
    \hline
  \end{tabularx}
\end{table}

    \hline
    Live Update Transport & $4\text{--}5\text{ s}$ HTTP auto-polling + focus refresh instead of WebSockets. & Stateless resilience across mobile Wi-Fi sleep/reconnect cycles with near-real-time UX. \\
    \hline
    Payment Settlement & Transparent VND fee computation without live banking gateway. & Financial billing is outside project scope. \\
